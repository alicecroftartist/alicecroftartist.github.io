A gaggle of gregarious greylag geese

❮ ❯

"A gaggle of gregarious greylag geese" depicts some mischevious geese I saw poking their heads out from a barley field on a sunset walk this summer.

This print is 45cm x 33cm including borders, in landscape format. It is printed on archival Fabriano Rosaspina paper using lightfast inks, ensuring your print stays just as beautiful for years to come.

All prints are sold unframed. If you wish for me to organise framing I am open to this on a case by case basis. Please contact me via email.

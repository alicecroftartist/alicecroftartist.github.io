Commissions

I accept a limited number of commissions each year, including wedding painting, bespoke prints and oil paintings. If you have a particular place, person, or memory you’d like captured in print or paint, I’d be delighted to discuss ideas, sizes, and timelines. My pricing below is a rough guide, but may be amended depending on the complexity of the work.

For example, A4 paper is 93.5 square inches, making an oil painting £139.50. However, if this painting included several faces or complex elements, this price may be increased to account for this.

I will always advise you which medium I believe is best and provide an accurate quote upon consultation.

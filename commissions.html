Commissions

I accept a limited number of commissions each year, including bespoke prints and oil paintings. If you have a particular place, person, or memory you’d like captured in print or paint, I’d be delighted to discuss ideas, sizes, and timelines.

To enquire about a commission, please email alicecroftartist@gmail.com with a brief description of what you have in mind, your budget, and any deadlines.
